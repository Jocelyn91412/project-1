C-Drama

A Little Background Info

Chinese dramas, commonly called C-dramas, have a long history influenced by Chinese literature, traditional storytelling, and television culture. Modern C-dramas became popular internationally through streaming platforms, bringing historical dramas, fantasy adventures, romance stories, and modern-day romances to audiences worldwide. Genres such as xianxia (fantasy), wuxia (martial arts), and historical palace dramas have helped Chinese television gain a large global following.

Most Popular C-Dramas Globally

  1. Pursuit of Jade

    Pursuit of Jade is a Chinese historical drama that combines romance, family conflicts, and political struggles. The story follows characters who must navigate complicated relationships while dealing with expectations from society and powerful families.

    The drama focuses on loyalty, ambition, and personal choices while exploring how characters balance love with responsibility.

  2. Love's Ambition

    Love's Ambition follows a successful television host and a wealthy businessman whose relationship becomes complicated by ambition, misunderstandings, and personal goals.

    The drama explores modern relationships, career pressure, social expectations, and the challenges of maintaining love while pursuing success.

  3. Hidden Love

    Hidden Love is a romantic coming-of-age drama about Sang Zhi, a young woman who develops feelings for Duan Jiaxu, a friend of her older brother.

    The series follows their journey from childhood admiration to an adult relationship while exploring themes of growth, friendship, and first love.
